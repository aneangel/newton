Raise the OpenGL viewer's default near plane from 0.01 m to 0.05 m, matching `Camera.MIN_PIVOT_DISTANCE`. The 100000:1 near/far ratio exhausted the 24-bit depth buffer, and kilometre-scale ground planes such as the 2000 km plane Isaac Lab's terrain importer spawns stopped rasterising entirely, so objects standing on them were occluded incorrectly.
